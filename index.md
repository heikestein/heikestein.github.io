You've landed on the website of Heike Stein's lab. We are a [team of computational neuroscientists](https://heikestein.github.io/team) at Sorbonne Université in Paris. What brings us together is our goal to understand neural computation by studying how neural activity patterns relate to behavioral dynamics.

To learn more about our science, please find an overview of our main current and previous [lines of research](https://heikestein.github.io/projects) and download our [papers](https://heikestein.github.io/papers) (you can also find recorded talks, openly available data, and code). If you're a neuroscientist interested in behavioral modeling, please check out [BAMB!](https://heikestein.github.io/bamb), a Barcelona-based summer school that Heike is co-organizing.
