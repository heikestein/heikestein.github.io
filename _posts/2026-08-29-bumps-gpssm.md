The head direction (HD) system consists of neurons that fire selectively as a function of an animal’s head orientation. By integrating
vestibular and visual signals, these cells maintain a stable internal sense of direction. Current circuit models of the HD system typically assume fixed functional forms for the dynamics of latent HD variables. Data-driven models, in contrast, recover latent states and dynamic trajectories without consideration of the known dynamical properties of this well-studied system.

In this project, we recover HD dynamics directly from data while preserving a mechanistic, circuit-grounded interpretation. We want to learn models that fulfill core criteria:
- data-driven: the dynamics are learned, not assumed.
- interpretable: each term maps to a biologically grounded circuit mechanism.
- modular: each input (vestibular, visual, olfactory, memory, …) influences HD dynamics through separate terms in the equations of latent dynamics.
