The head direction (HD) cells are
neurons that fire selectively as
a function of an animal’s head
orientation. By integrating
vestibular and visual signals,
these cells maintain a stable
internal sense of direction

In this project, we recover head direction dynamics directly from data while preserving a mechanistic, circuit-grounded interpretation. We want to learn models that fulfill core criteria:
- data-driven: the dynamics are learned, not assumed.
- interpretable: each term maps to a biologically grounded circuit mechanism.
- modular: each input (vestibular, visual, olfactory, memory, …) influences HD dynamics through separate terms in the equations of latent dynamics.
